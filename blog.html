Patient Education

Frequently Asked Health Questions

Common questions answered by our clinical advisory board.

How often should healthy adults schedule comprehensive blood work?

For healthy adults under 40 without chronic symptoms or familial risk factors, an annual comprehensive metabolic panel (CMP), lipid panel, complete blood count (CBC), and HbA1c is standard. Adults over 40 or those with hypertension or elevated BMI benefit from bi-annual reviews.

Are medical consultations conducted over telehealth as thorough as in-person visits?

Telehealth is highly effective for routine follow-ups, medication adjustments, dermatological triage, lab result interpretations, and mental healthcare. If physical palpation, emergency auscultation, or diagnostic imaging is required, our clinicians will immediately coordinate an in-clinic appointment.

How does Stackly Healthcare ensure patient data privacy under HIPAA?

All communications, electronic health records (EHR), and telehealth sessions utilize 256-bit AES end-to-end encryption. Only authenticated medical practitioners assigned to your direct care team have authorized clearance to review your clinical records.

What is the protocol for obtaining an emergency prescription refill?

Patients can log into their Stackly Portal, navigate to Prescriptions & Rx, and submit a 1-click refill request. Our on-duty pharmacist and primary care team review requests within 2 hours, sending digital prescriptions directly to your local pharmacy.
