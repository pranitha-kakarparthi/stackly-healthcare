Visitor Information

Inpatient Visiting Hours & Campus Guidelines

To ensure optimal rest and infection control for recovering patients, we request all visitors to adhere to our scheduled visiting hours:

  • • General Inpatient Wards: 11:00 AM – 01:00 PM & 05:00 PM – 07:00 PM
  • • Intensive Care Units (ICU/CCU): 04:30 PM – 05:30 PM (1 Attendant only)
  • • Pediatric & NICU: Parents permitted 24/7 with sterile gown protocol

Safety Guidelines for Visitors

1. Wear sanitized medical masks in all clinical corridors.
2. Children below 12 years are not permitted inside intensive care zones for safety.
3. Outside food and unsealed floral arrangements are strictly restricted to maintain hospital sanitation standards.
