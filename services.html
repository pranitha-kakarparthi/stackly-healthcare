Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions regarding appointments, insurance, or emergency services? Find your answers below.

How do I book an in-person or virtual consultation?

You can create an account via our Patient Portal, select your preferred specialist and time slot, or call our 24/7 helpline at +91 9876543210.

Do you support cashless health insurance policies?

Yes, Stackly has established cashless network tie-ups with over 40 leading public and private health insurance providers and TPAs.

What should I do in an emergency situation?

Our Salem Emergency Trauma Center operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Call our immediate ambulance dispatch line or report directly to our Emergency Bay at MMR Complex, Chinna Thirupathi.

Can I access my lab test results online?

Yes! Once your pathology or imaging tests are verified by our radiologists, you can immediately view and download reports through your Patient Dashboard.
