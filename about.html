Our Founding Story

Rooted in Clinical Integrity, Driven by Patient Hope

Established as an ambitious multi-specialty institution in Salem, Stackly was built on the core belief that no patient should have to travel across continents to access world-class surgical care and precision diagnostics.

Over the last thirty years, we have grown from a modest 50-bed community clinic into a comprehensive 300-bed tertiary care hospital equipped with hybrid operating theaters, dedicated cardiac ICUs, and advanced robotic surgery consoles.

Community Mission

Bringing Free Healthcare to Rural Salem Districts

True to our founding values, Stackly conducts weekly mobile medical camps and cardiovascular screening drives for underserved communities across Salem district and surrounding villages.

In the last calendar year alone, over 18,000 complimentary blood glucose, blood pressure, and vision screenings were performed by our volunteer clinicians.
