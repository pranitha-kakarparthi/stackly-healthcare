Pharmacological Safety, Storage & Clinical Adherence Protocols

Certified Guidelines
🌡️ Storage Recommendations

Keep all cardiovascular medication in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight (below 25°C). Never store tablets inside bathroom medicine cabinets due to moisture fluctuations.

⏰ Missed Dose Protocol

If you miss a scheduled dose by more than 4 hours, do not take a double dose. Resume normal scheduled timing at your next interval and record the event in your patient portal log.
