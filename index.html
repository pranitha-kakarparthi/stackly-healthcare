Robotic Precision

Minimally Invasive Robotic Surgery & Sub-Millimeter Diagnostics

Our Salem surgical suites feature 4th-generation robotic surgical consoles and integrated 3T MRI intraoperative telemetry. By minimizing incision dimensions, our patients experience significantly faster postoperative recovery, minimal blood loss, and reduced hospital stays.

  • ✓ Sub-millimeter Surgical Accuracy: 3D high-magnification stereoscopic visualization for delicate cardiac and spinal interventions.
  • ✓ Rapid Ambulatory Mobilization: 85% of orthopedic patients achieve assisted walking within 24–48 hours post-op.
